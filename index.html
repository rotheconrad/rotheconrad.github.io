About

Computational genomics for teams building serious biological infrastructure.

Roth E. Conrad is a Research Scientist with the USDA-ARS Genomics & Bioinformatics Research Unit at the University of Georgia, associated with CAGT and IPBGG. His work sits at the intersection of computational genomics, pangenomics, comparative genomics, scientific AI systems, and open-source bioinformatics infrastructure.

His publication history and early open-source work span microbial evolution, environmental genomics, metagenomics, population genetics, and comparative genomics. That foundation now informs plant pangenomics, graph genomics, long-read sequencing strategy, plant breeding genomics, and AI-assisted scientific workflows. The throughline is systems-level judgment: turning messy biological evidence into reproducible analyses, durable tools, and decisions that research and breeding teams can trust.

GitHub / Open Source

Practical software for reproducible genomics.

Roth develops open-source workflows and analysis utilities for comparative genomics, metagenomics, assembly review, marker validation, pangenome exploration, and scientific AI infrastructure. The emphasis is on tools that are inspectable, maintainable, and usable by collaborators working with real biological questions.
